What went wrong, and why

Dubecor wants quote requests for building work. This year the ads mostly reached people who weren't ready, didn't fit, or were looking for something else. And the results got worse as the year went on.

On both channels

1

We never found out which leads became customers

In September 2025 we agreed with Jonathan to track which leads became real quotes and jobs. He would fill in a sheet each month, and we would feed it back to Meta and Google.

The first sheet only came in February 2026, after many reminders, and it was clear it wouldn't be kept up monthly. So we didn't finish the link.

Result: Meta and Google never learned which leads are worth money, and kept chasing volume instead of quality.

2

Most of the budget went to Google, the more expensive channel

In September 2025 Jonathan asked to put the most money on Google:

“€500 Meta Ads en €1000 Google Ads ipv €800/€700”

Mike argued against it, because the figures pointed the other way (“spreekt dit de cijfers wat tegen”), and proposed more for Meta. Google stayed the main channel and got 70% of the budget from June 2026.

Result: Meta dropped to €300 a month, and Meta requests dropped to zero.

Meta Ads

3

The calculator brought many leads, but the wrong ones

133 calculator entries via Meta, at a few euros each. But many didn't fit. Jonathan, 03/03/2026:

“wordt deels ingevuld door contacten die voor ons niet relevant zijn (te weinig budget, locatie te ver)”

He asked to spend less on it, and in May to switch those campaigns off altogether.

4

Calculator leads got only one follow-up mail

A series of mails over 30 days was planned. It was brought back to one mail because of other priorities and budget, and because Jonathan was convinced these leads weren't the right ones.

Result: people who were curious but not ready yet never heard from Dubecor again.

5

Since August, Meta mainly sold projects, not building work

Meta brought 425 brochure downloads for Dubecor's own projects, at €2,59 each. A good result for selling homes.

But for building work, the requests Jonathan measures us on, Meta has had almost no budget since then.

6

Meta didn't stick to the target age group

Meta treats the age range only as a suggestion. In the Branding campaign, 37% of the budget went to people over 65.

Google Ads

7

The switch to ruwbouw made Google worse

Before · AannemerJan–Jun 20266 forms€2.319,80 · 589 clicks
After · RuwbouwJul–15 Nov 20260 forms€2.940,87 · 682 clicks

People still click as often, but nobody fills in a form any more. The positioning suits Dubecor. But “totaalaannemer” is only searched by people looking for a contractor, while “metser”, “metselwerken” and “beton” are mostly searched by others.

Since July, every Google request came from people who searched “Dubecor” by name.

8

We bought the wrong search words

“Metser”, “metselaar”, “metselwerken” and “betonplaat” are searched by do-it-yourselfers, price comparers and job seekers. Over half of the traceable budget went to searches like:

metser gezochtzwembad metselenbetonplaat voor tuinhuisruwbouw prijs per m²

We excluded 29 of these searches (24 on 22/10, 5 more on 05/11), such as jobs, buyers and small jobs. That wasn't enough: words like “metser” and “betonplaat” match so many different searches that new do-it-yourself variants kept coming in.

Excluding searches only removes what we have already seen. The keywords themselves keep attracting the wrong people.

9

The landing page didn't convince, so clicks were expensive

Until 19/10 the ads went to the homepage, then to /ruwbouw. Google rates that page “below average”. Only 1 in 3 visitors stayed, and a click cost €5 to €8.

What we propose

  1. Close the loop in a way that works for Dubecor. A 15-minute call each month to go through the leads, or a simple status per lead (good / not good / customer) in Jonathan's own system.
  2. Put the budget where requests are cheapest. More back to Meta for building work. Google only for “Dubecor” searches and a few strong words.
  3. Google: keep the ruwbouw focus, but use words from people who want work done. Stop the broad words and send visitors to the new ruwbouw pages.
  4. Meta: separate campaigns for project sales and building work, each with its own budget. Keep calculator leads within the work region.
  5. Check every change after 4–6 weeks against what we expected.
